OBJECTIVE

Reconstruct the complete relationship structure of every source within each sugya.
Do not redefine boundaries, reconstruct arguments from scratch, or perform full
Hebrew and grammatical exegesis.

ANALYSIS RULES

1. Include every source recognized by the authority analysis and every source
   materially used by the argument analysis. Verify all entries against the bundle.

2. Preserve meaningful source groups from the authority analysis unless the bundle
   proves that a grouping is mistaken.

3. For every source, map:
   - the correct argument number;
   - the precise position, question, objection, distinction, application, or
     resolution affected;
   - authority role and primary role;
   - why this specific source was chosen;
   - its relationship to the argument;
   - its relationship to the sugya;
   - interactions with other sources;
   - direct dependency;
   - effect if removed;
   - replacement possibility;
   - confidence.

4. Relationship explanations must answer HOW. Identify:
   - what immediately precedes the source;
   - the concrete claim or problem it addresses;
   - the source's exact contribution;
   - the inference enabled, blocked, narrowed, or resolved;
   - why the argument would be weaker, different, or incomplete without it.

5. Choose the most precise relationship type allowed by the standards.
   Do not use “supports” when the source actually limits, distinguishes, reframes,
   contextualizes, illustrates, applies, or resolves.

6. Use relationship_analysis.primary_explanation for the main causal explanation.
   Use up to three additional_explanations only when the source contributes in
   materially different ways.

7. Record source_interactions only when two sources directly affect one another's
   interpretation or argumentative force. Do not create interactions merely because
   two sources appear in the same sugya.

8. Record only direct dependency. Distinguish dependency from interaction.

9. When the same citation performs materially different functions, create separate
   entries only when needed to preserve those distinct functions.

10. Do not duplicate detailed Hebrew, grammar, lexical analysis, or full rabbinic
    interpretation. Those belong to Exegesis Analysis.

11. Complete the sugya-level relationship synthesis defined by the schema, including
    the source sequence, major dependencies, interaction pattern, and how the source
    network produces or constrains the conclusion.

FINAL CHECK

Verify that:
- every authority and argument source is accounted for;
- every source is connected to the correct argument and position;
- dependency and interaction are not conflated;
- no explanation is generic;
- every schema field is preserved.
